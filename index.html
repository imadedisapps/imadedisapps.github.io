Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Overview

Loop ("the app") is a local reminder and notification scheduling app developed by I Made Dis. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

Loop does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

All reminder data (times, dates, messages) is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

Internet Access

Loop does not require or use an internet connection. The app functions entirely offline.

Third-Party Services

Loop does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Permissions

Loop requests the following Android permissions, all used solely for core functionality:

PermissionPurpose
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMSchedule reminders at the exact requested time
POST_NOTIFICATIONSDisplay reminder notifications
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRe-schedule reminders after device restart
WAKE_LOCKEnsure reminders fire in battery-saving mode

No permission is used to collect, access, or transmit personal information.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored reminders.

Children's Privacy

Loop does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
